# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy
and kindness; respecting differing opinions and experiences; giving and gracefully accepting
constructive feedback; accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes;
and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery; trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments; public or private harassment; publishing others' private information
without permission; and conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior through the private maintainer
contact published with releases. Maintainers will review and investigate reports promptly and
fairly, protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and may remove comments, reject a
contribution, impose a temporary ban, or permanently ban a participant.

Maintainers who do not act consistently with this Code of Conduct may be reported to the
project owners through the same private contact.
